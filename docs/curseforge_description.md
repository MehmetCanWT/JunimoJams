# 🌿 Junimo Jams — Universal In-Game Music HUD & Media Player Hub

**Junimo Jams** brings a modern, ultra-responsive, and customizable music player overlay directly into your **Stardew Valley** farm! Whether you are harvesting crops, diving into the Skull Cavern, or fishing at the beach, control and monitor your real-life music playback without ever having to Alt-Tab out of the game.

Powered by a zero-GC high-performance rendering pipeline, Junimo Jams runs at a locked **60+ FPS** with zero frame drops, stutter, or lag.

---

## ✨ Features at a Glance

* 🎵 **Universal Music Player Support**: Works automatically with **Spotify**, **YouTube Music** (Chrome, Firefox, Brave, Edge, Opera, etc.), **Apple Music**, **TIDAL**, **Deezer**, **Cider**, **VLC**, **foobar2000**, and many more!
* ⚡ **True Plug & Play (Zero Extra Setup)**:
  * **Linux / Steam Deck / Proton**: Connects directly to your desktop's media session via fast D-Bus Unix domain sockets. No Wine hacks or extra programs needed!
  * **Windows**: Works seamlessly with system media controls and background players.
  * **macOS**: Built-in native AppleScript & media control integration.
* 🖼️ **Album Cover Art & Dynamic Fallbacks**: Automatically fetches high-resolution album artwork for currently playing tracks or displays a clean animated icon when offline.
* 📜 **Butter-Smooth Sub-Pixel Marquee**: Long song titles and artist names glide smoothly with continuous linear pixel scrolling and gentle pauses at each end—no jerky character jumping!
* 🎛️ **Full Interactive Controls**:
  * Click **Play / Pause**, **Previous Track**, and **Next Track** directly on the HUD overlay.
  * Live animated equalizer bars that bounce while music is playing.
  * Slim progress bar with current elapsed and total track duration.
* 🎨 **10 Handcrafted Visual Themes**:
  * **Pelican Classic** (Warm authentic Stardew wood & parchment tone)
  * **Shadcn Zinc Dark** (Modern minimalist dark UI)
  * **Tokyo Night** (Neon aesthetic Japanese night vibe)
  * **Nord Frost** (Crisp arctic blues)
  * **Cyberpunk Neon** (Vibrant synthwave magenta & cyan)
  * **Earthy Green** (Organic woodland moss aesthetic)
  * **Midnight OLED** (Pitch-black battery & eye saver)
  * And more!
* ⚙️ **In-Game Customization (GMCM Support)**:
  * Change **HUD Scale** freely from **0.5x to 2.5x** to match any monitor resolution (1080p, 1440p, 4K, or handheld Steam Deck).
  * Reposition anywhere on screen with custom X / Y coordinates.
  * Toggle album art, playback buttons, marquee text, or equalizer.
  * Press **`F6`** anytime to open the in-game Music Hub menu!

---

## 🎮 How to Use in Game

* **HUD Overlay**: Displayed automatically in the top-left corner (customizable position).
* **Media Buttons**: Click Previous, Play/Pause, or Next directly with your mouse.
* **Open Music Hub Menu**: Press **`F6`** (can be remapped in config) to open the full interactive music modal.
* **Configure Settings**: Use the **Generic Mod Config Menu** button on the title screen or in the pause menu to adjust scale, position, themes, and shortcuts in real time.

## 📋 Requirements

1. **[Stardew Valley](https://www.stardewvalley.net/)** (version 1.6 or higher)
2. **[SMAPI](https://smapi.io/)** (version 4.1.0 or higher)
3. **[RustedUI Framework](https://www.curseforge.com/stardewvalley/mods/rustedui)** (required UI dependency)
4. *(Optional)* **Generic Mod Config Menu (GMCM)** for in-game configuration UI.

---

## 📥 Installation

1. Install the latest version of **SMAPI**.
2. Download and install **RustedUI Framework** into your `Stardew Valley/Mods` folder.
3. Download **Junimo Jams** and extract the zip file.
4. Place the `JunimoJams` folder into your `Stardew Valley/Mods` directory.
5. Launch the game through SMAPI and enjoy your tunes!
